Help and support

Questions, a wrong answer in the quiz, or something not working? Email support@familyquiz.app and we'll get back to you.

How to play

Add the players and their ages, pick a topic and a difficulty, then pass the phone round. Each player gets questions that suit their age. The quiz master taps Show answer, then Correct or Wrong. Answering straight away scores 2 points; using Show choices scores 1.

Playing on several phones

Choose Everyone on their own phone on one phone and share the code. Others join with the code in the app or at familyquiz.app.

Your own questions

Paste a link to a Google Sheet in Edit players. The sheet must be shared as "Anyone with the link can view".

Deleting your account

Open your account in the app and choose Delete my account. See Privacy for what is stored.
