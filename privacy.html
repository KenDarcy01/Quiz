Privacy

Family Quiz is made by Kenneth Darcy. This page explains what the app and the website at familyquiz.app store, and why. Last updated 25 November 2026.

Playing without an account

A pass-the-phone game needs no account. Player names, ages, scores, settings and which questions each player has had are kept only on your device. When you host a game for other phones, the game, its players' names and their answers are stored on our server so the phones can follow along. A guest id with no personal details is created for this.

With an account

If you sign up, we store your email address, your name, and the profiles you add (a name and an age band for each, such as "8-9" or "adult"), plus the games you play and their scores. We use this only to run the app: signing you in, picking questions that suit each player's age, and keeping scores.

Children

Children can play on a parent's phone or in a profile a parent creates. A child's profile is a first name or nickname and an age band. Children cannot create accounts or give an email address. There is no chat with strangers.

What we don't do

No advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, no analytics services, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts, profiles, hosted games and the question bank.
  • Apple's iTunes Search, on the website only, is asked for a song preview when a music question comes up. It receives the search words only.
  • Google Sheets, only if you paste in a link to your own question sheet.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to your account and choose Delete my account. That removes your account, every profile in it and your games straight away. Data kept only on your device is removed when you delete the app. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.
